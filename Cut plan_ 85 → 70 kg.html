Rules

  • Weigh yourself every morning and track the weekly average.
  • Adjust every 2 weeks. Losing less than 0.3 kg/week: cut 100–150 kcal. Losing more than 0.8 kg/week, or legs feel dead: add about 200 kcal.
  • Keep strength training 1–2 times a week.
  • Consider a 1–2 week maintenance break at about 78 kg.
